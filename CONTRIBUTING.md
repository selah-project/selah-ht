# Patisipe nan tradiksyon kreyòl ayisyen Selah la

Mèsi — w ap ede tradiksyon sa a vin pi klè, pi vizib, e pi pre kreyòl tout bon an. Ou pa bezwen se yon save: di sa ou wè, bay rezon ou, epi separe sa ou sèten de sa ou sèlman sipoze.

## Rapòte oswa pwopoze

- Si yon mo mande diskisyon, oswa si li gen plis pase yon fason pou li l — louvri yon **Issue**.
- Si fot la klè epi koreksyon an klè tou — louvri yon **Pull request**.
- Depo a: <https://github.com/selah-project/selah-ht>
- Pou kesyon sou lojisyèl la oswa sou sekirite — <support@selahproject.com> oswa [Selah Support](https://selahproject.com/support).

## Sa pou mete nan rapò a

Liv la, chapit la, vèsè a; mòso ebre a ki konsène; tèks ki la kounye a; tèks ou pwopoze a; rezon chanjman an; epi baz ou a — diksyonè, gramè, kontèks oswa edisyon. Di nou tou si ou pale kreyòl depi timoun epi si ou li ebre dirèk.

## Règ pou modifye fichye yo

Fichye yo gen fòm sa a: `<liv>/<chapit>/<vèsè>.json`.

- Si chanjman an manyen tou de — `translation` la ak `gloss` ki koresponn nan chanje ansanm.
- `book`, `chapter`, `verse`, `ref`, `surface` ebre a, ansanm ak lòd ak kantite tokèn yo — **pa janm chanje yo**. Kase aliyman an se pi gwo fot la.
- **Swiv Tab Non yo**: יהוה → **Yawe**; אלהים → **Elohim**; אל → **El**; אלוה → **Eloah**; אדני → **Adònay**; שדי → **Chadday**; יה → **Yah**; שאול → **Cheol**; חסד → **hesed**; משיח → **Machiya**. Nan chèz Non an, nou pa aksepte **Seyè / Seyè a**, **Letènèl / Etènèl la**, **Granmèt / Mèt la**, **Jewova / Jeova**, ni **Bondye / Bondye a**. (Pou mo òdinè *elohim* la — dye yo, dye nasyon yo, zidòl yo — desizyon an fèt tokèn pa tokèn, ak **ti lèt**. Pou mèt ki se moun — *mèt mwen*, *chèf mwen* — se la *mèt* la kòrèk.)
- **Pa gen prefiks *Bondye* sou fòm nou yo**: ekri **Yawe**, pa *Bondye Yawe*; ekri **Elohim**, pa *Bondye Elohim*. Non an rete yon non.
- Non moun ak non kote rete nan fòm ebre a: **Moche**, **Aharòn**, **Yehochoua**, **Chaoul**, **Mitsrayim**, **Paro**, **Yerouchalayim**, **Avraham**, **Tsiyòn**. **Cheol** pa janm «lanfè»; **Machiya** pa janm «Kris» ni «Mesi».
- Kenbe mak **⟨את⟩** la ak tout ajou ki nan ⟨kwochè ang⟩; pa efase yo an silans. Sa ki anndan kwochè yo se **kreyòl**.
- Deyò kwochè yo — kreyòl pwòp: pa gen rès angle ni franse, pa gen lèt ki soti nan yon lòt alfabè (eksepte ⟨את⟩), pa gen HTML.

## Òtograf

Se **òtograf ofisyèl kreyòl la** (IPN, 1980) ki mande, san eksepsyon nan tèks kouran an:

- Pa gen `c`, `q`, `x` pou kont yo; pa gen `ç`, `ph`, `th`, `eau`, `oi`. Ekri **k**, **s**, **f**, **t**, **o**, **wa**.
- **`ch` = /ʃ/** — se grafèm sa a ki pote son ebre ש la. **Pa janm `sh`**, ki se ekriti angle oswa franse.
- **`ou` = /u/**, **`w` = /w/**, **`y` = /j/**, **`j` = /ʒ/**, **`g` toujou di**, **`s` toujou /s/** menm ant de vwayèl (Yitshak).
- Aksan grav la kase nazalizasyon an: **Adònay** (pa *Adonay*), **El Elyòn**, **Tsiyòn**, **Aharòn**, **Chimòn**, **Edèn**, **Kenaàn**.
- **`ng`** se yon grafèm: separe ak yon tirè nan non ebre yo — **Èn-Gedi**, pa *Engedi*.
- Doub konsòn franse yo tonbe: **Yawe-Nisi**, pa *Nissi*.
- `h` ekri kote kreyòl ka son l, ant de vwayèl: **Elohim**, **Eloah**, **Avraham**, **Yehochoua**. Li pa ekri devan yon lòt konsòn: se poutèt sa **Yawe**, pa *Yahwe*. Nan **Yah**, `h` final la rete kòm mak lèt ה a.
- **`kh`** se yon digram make pou כ dous la, sèlman nan mo ebre.

## Prensip yo

Ebre a anvan tout. Si de lekti kanpe toude, esplike diferans lan — pa prezante yon preferans tankou yon sèten. Pa kopye tradiksyon modèn ki gen dwadotè. Literalite pase anvan fasilite lekti: nan vèsè ki di yo, tèks la mete toutouni.

## Travay ki fèt ak entèlijans atifisyèl

Si ou sèvi ak yon gwo modèl lang oswa yon tradiksyon machin — di l aklè, epi mete pwòp verifikasyon pa ou akote l. Pa voye anpil tèks ki poko tcheke. Chak mo ou pwopoze rete responsablite ou.

## Lisans, kredi ak revizyon

Lè ou patisipe, ou dakò pou tout sa ki aksepte pibliye anba [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE.md). Istwa Git la kite rejis la ak rezon yo louvri pou tout moun. Moun k ap tcheke yo konpare pwopozisyon an ak ebre a, ak ray yo, ak referans yo, epi ak aliyman an — yo ka aksepte l, travay li ansanm avè ou, tann plis baz, oswa refize l ak yon rezon. Se tèks la ki anba kritik, se pa moun nan.

# Contributing to the Selah Haitian Creole rendering (English)

Thank you — you are helping this rendering become clearer, more visible, and closer to real kreyòl. You do not need to be a scholar: say what you saw, give your grounds, and separate what you are sure of from what you are guessing.

- **Issue** for a word that needs discussion or has more than one reading; **Pull request** when both the error and the fix are clear. Repository: <https://github.com/selah-project/selah-ht>. Software or security questions: <support@selahproject.com>.
- Files are `<book>/<chapter>/<verse>.json`. Change `translation` and the matching `gloss` together. **Never** change `book`, `chapter`, `verse`, `ref`, the Hebrew `surface`, or the order and count of the tokens — breaking the alignment is the costliest error.
- **The Name table**: יהוה → **Yawe**; אלהים → **Elohim**; אל → **El**; אלוה → **Eloah**; אדני → **Adònay**; שדי → **Chadday**; יה → **Yah**; שאול → **Cheol**; חסד → **hesed**; משיח → **Machiya**. At the Name seat, *Seyè*, *Letènèl / Etènèl*, *Granmèt / Mèt*, *Jewova*, and *Bondye* are rejected. Common-noun *elohim* (the gods of the nations, idols) is decided token by token and stays **lowercase**; human masters take *mèt*.
- **No honorific prefix** on our forms: **Yawe**, not *Bondye Yawe*; **Elohim**, not *Bondye Elohim*.
- Keep the **⟨את⟩** marker and the ⟨angle-bracket⟩ supplied words; never drop them silently. Inside the brackets — kreyòl only. Outside the brackets — clean kreyòl: no English or French residue, no letters from another script (except ⟨את⟩), no HTML.
- **Official kreyòl orthography (IPN, 1980)**, no French spellings. In particular **`ch`, never `sh`**, for ש — so *Moche*, *Cheol*, *Chadday*, *Yerouchalayim* — and the grave accent that breaks nasalization: *Adònay*, *Tsiyòn*, *Aharòn*, *Kenaàn*.
- Hebrew first. If two readings both stand, explain the difference; do not present a preference as a certainty. Do not copy copyrighted modern translations. Literalness outranks readability: at the hard verses the text is laid bare.
- If you used a large language model or machine translation, say so plainly and put your own review beside it. Do not send bulk unreviewed text.
- By contributing you agree that what is accepted is published under [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE.md). Reviewers compare every proposal against the Hebrew, the rails, the references, and the alignment. Criticize the text, not the person.

## Conduct

Be honest, be kind, show your evidence. Distinguish certainty from
suggestion. The maintainers weigh and decide.
